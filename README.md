# Thaumaturge Tinker

Addon for **Thaumaturge** on **Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge)**, bringing classic Thaumic Tinkerer & KAMI content to modern Minecraft.

## Links

- **CurseForge**: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/thaumaturge-tinker

## Features

- **Osmotic Enchanter & Custom Enchantments**: Empower tools and armor with vis-fueled enchanting and unique enchantments (Ascent Boost, Auto-Smelt, Disintegrate, Pounce, Shockwave, Tunnel, Vampirism, Valiance, and more).
- **Wand & Caster Foci**: Smelting, Flight, Deflection, Telekinesis, Dislocation, Ender Chest, Healing, Shadowbeam, XP Drain, and Recall foci.
- **Ichor & KAMI Progression**: Ichor, Ichorcloth, Ichorium tools, Awakened Ichorium tools & armor, Placement Mirror, Black Hole Talisman, Feline Amulet, Protoclay, Sky Pearl, and Warp Gates.
- **Artifice & Automation**: Transvector Interface & Dislocator, Kinetic & Corporeal Attractors, Tablet of Necromancy, Animation Tablet, Levitator, Tool Dynamism, and Repairer.
- **Elemental Fires & Infused Crops**: Elemental fires (`Aer`, `Ignis`, `Aqua`, `Terra`, `Ordo`, `Perditio`), Infused Seeds, Grains, and Imbued Potions.

## Requirements

- **Minecraft**: `26.1.2`
- **NeoForge**: `26.1.2.122+`
- **Thaumaturge**: `1.1.0+`
- **Optional**: Curios API, Jade, JEI / REI

## Building from Source

```bash
./gradlew build
```

The compiled mod JAR will be located in `build/libs/`.
